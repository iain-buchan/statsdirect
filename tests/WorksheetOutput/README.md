# Checks of output frames written to a picked sheet

A console project that drives the real document host: it opens the main window off screen, without start-up, settings writes, IPC or update dialogs, makes two grids, and calls the host's frame output as the template processor does for an `output-frame` step. The checks are made on a build of the program, as those of `tests/InteractiveRWindows` are: `StatsDirect.dll` of the Release configuration, or of the folder given with `-p:StatsDirectBin=...`.

```
dotnet run -c Release
```

Set `STATSDIRECT_WORKSHEET_OUTPUT_ARTIFACTS` to a folder to have the results written there as `results.json`.

**What is checked.** With the grid of the data active, as it is when the follow-on of a regression is run, and a sheet of another grid picked for the output: the first frame of a step that keeps the selection (`keep-selection="true"`, as the fits and residuals of a multiple linear regression and the fits of LOESS are written) goes to the picked sheet, and the grid of the data is untouched; the frames after it follow it on the picked sheet without adding a sheet; a pick of a new sheet makes one sheet for an operation's frames, not one for each; without a pick a step that keeps the selection still writes after the selection of the active grid; and a pick made afresh, as a later run of the operation makes, is taken again. A new grid takes the workbook font saved in the preferences at its own size, whether that size was saved with a comma or with a point as the decimal separator (5.3.9 and earlier wrote it in the culture of the day, and 10,5 points were read as 105 where the separator is a point), and a font chosen where the separator is a comma is saved with a point. The "Precision of estimates:" prompt of logistic and of Poisson regression, prepared from the operation's definition and read by the main window, takes a precision of 0.00000000001 typed into its box in full (up to 5.3.9 the two definitions limited the box to 12 characters, and the 13 typed were cut at 0.0000000000, a precision of 0). A recent file that could not be opened stays in the recent files when its volume is not there, such as a USB stick not plugged in, and leaves them when its volume is there (up to 5.3.9 choosing a file on an absent drive from the list took it off the list); the path on the absent volume is only named, and the list of the preferences is put back.

**What these checks showed when they were written (1 November 2026).** The host took the selection a step keeps before the sheet the user picked, so the fits and residuals of a multiple linear regression went into the workbook of the data after the current selection whatever sheet was picked, and the pick was ignored; the pick now comes first, and the frames after the first follow it.
